Ink line-art Short, 10 s, black strokes on off-white, one blue accent:
Why is the sky blue? A sun outline draws itself; a red wavy ray and a
blue wavy ray travel right through a cluster of tiny air molecules;
from 4.4 s the blue light scatters in every direction toward a stick
figure looking up.
Captions: "Why is the sky blue?" /
"Air scatters short blue light far more than red".
