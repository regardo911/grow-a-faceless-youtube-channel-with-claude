Painterly Short, 10 s: Why is the sea salty? Cream paper with fine
grain made in code (seeded, so it never flickers). Soft washes: brown
hills on the left, a blue sea on the right. From 1.5 s light rain
falls; from 3 s a river paints itself from the hills to the sea and
white salt specks ride it out.
Captions: "Why is the sea salty?" / "Rain wears down rock and rivers
carry the salt out to sea".
