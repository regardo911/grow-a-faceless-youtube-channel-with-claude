## Style sheet (every Short)
- Background: navy #0b1026 to #141c45 with a sparse star field.
  One subject at a time, drawn in flat 2D or toon-shaded 3D.
- Palette: ocean #2f7ed8, land #5cb85c, Sun #ffb02e, Moon #e8e2cf,
  accent yellow #ffd166 (one keyword per caption only). On a yellow
  subject, the keyword is white.
- Type: Archivo Black. Captions white, 68 px, 4 words at most per
  group, each group on screen at least 1.0 s (a group may start a
  moment after its first word to get there), top at y = 1000, right
  padding 220 px (clear of the Shorts buttons).
- Framing: the subject inside the middle 80% of the frame with a
  margin; nothing important behind the caption band; use the full
  height; every label fully on screen.
- Frame 0 shows the subject and the question. Something changes on
  screen at least every 3 seconds.
- Motion: snappy 0.3 s for pops, smooth 1.5 s for moves, slow drifts.
- Truth: every number on screen has a source; every caption has a
  frame that shows it; physics checked in one written line.
- Banned: a centred title on a gradient, everything fading in,
  corner labels, the same opening shot as the last video.
- Match the look of refs/*.png.
