Make channel 2's first Short from script.txt and timing.json, by the
style sheet. A side cross-section of a pin tumbler lock: five stacks
of pins pressed down by springs, a brass plug, a dashed orange line
where plug meets body. The right key slides in and lifts each stack.
Mechanism rule: at rest the upper pins cross into the plug and block
it; the right key lifts every gap exactly to the line; the wrong key
leaves one pin across it.
