## Style sheet (every Short)
- Background: warm grey #eceae4. Linework: navy #1b2333, 6 px
  outlines. One moving part in orange #ff7a1a; everything else flat
  greys and brass #e8c46a.
- Views: side cross-sections and exploded views; label every named
  part with a leader line.
- Type: Archivo Black. Captions navy, 66 px, 4 words at most per
  group, each group on screen at least 1.0 s (a group may start a
  moment after its first word to get there), top at y = 1000, right
  padding 220 px.
- Frame 0 shows the machine and the question.
- Truth: write the mechanism as one rule in the prompt ("the right
  key lifts every stack to the shear line") and check the motion
  against it.
- Banned: a centred title on a gradient, everything fading in,
  corner labels, the same opening shot as the last video.
