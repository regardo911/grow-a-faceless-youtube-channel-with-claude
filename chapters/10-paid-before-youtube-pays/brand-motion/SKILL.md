# Northlight Tutoring motion guidelines
Colours: primary #1E1B4B, text #F5F3FF, accent #F97316 (only the one
word that matters).
Type: Montserrat 900 for headlines, Inter 700 for body.
Logo: assets/logo.svg. Always the real file. Never redraw it.
Motion: calm and precise. Damped springs, slight overshoot. No
bouncy cartoon motion, no spinning, no glitch effects.
End card: logo + "Book a free lesson", held 2 seconds.
