## Channel brief (my channel; read this before every video)
- Niche: space and Earth science, explained by showing how the
  thing works, with "what if" hooks.
- Viewer: curious teens and adults on a phone, often watching muted.
- Shorts: 45 to 55 seconds, 1080x1920. That is 130 to 150 words
  in my voice.
- Voice: Kokoro am_michael through npx -y hyperframes@0.9.64 tts,
  one clip per line, 0.2 s between lines.
- Script rules: talk to the viewer as "you". Short sentences.
  No colons. Numbers written the way they are said. Line 1 is the
  hook and asks the title question. The last line pays it off.
- Facts: every factual line needs a source I can open (NASA, NOAA,
  a university, a peer-reviewed paper) in sources.md. No source,
  no line. The fact check runs last, after every rewrite.
- Never say: "mind-blowing", "you won't believe",
  "scientists are baffled", "in this video".
- My angle: I explain the mechanism on screen, so the viewer sees
  why, not just what.
