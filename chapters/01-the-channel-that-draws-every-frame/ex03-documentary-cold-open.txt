Make the cold open of a long-form YouTube documentary,
"The Night the Moon Disappeared". 1920x1080.
1. Voice the script in script.txt with npx -y hyperframes@0.9.64 tts,
   voice am_michael, speed 0.85. Tell me the length.
2. Get word timings with npx -y hyperframes@0.9.64 transcribe
   narr.wav --json.
3. Build the scenes on those timings: night sky and Moon, the Moon
   fading out, the sky going dark, Earth from above with its two tidal
   bulges, a bar chart (tide today vs Sun only), then a title card with
   three chapter chips.
4. Captions from the timings, 3 to 5 words at a time, near the bottom.
5. Check, then render to opener.mp4.
